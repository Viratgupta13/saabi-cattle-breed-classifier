How it works

A pretrained image network, adapted to Indian breeds, with checks that tell you when to doubt it.

01

See

The photo is resized and passed through MobileNetV2, a compact network pretrained on ImageNet and fine-tuned on labelled breed photos.

02

Decide

A softmax layer scores each breed. Temperature scaling calibrates these scores so “80%” is closer to meaning 80%.

03

Doubt

A feature-space check flags images unlike any known breed, and a confidence threshold flags borderline calls instead of guessing.

Honest about its limits

A good model is one that tells you where it struggles.

Where it struggles

  • Gir and Sahiwal look alike and are the most commonly confused pair.
  • Trained on roughly 1,000 images, so unusual angles or crossbreeds can fool it.
  • Only the 8 listed breeds are known; others will be mapped to the nearest one or flagged.

How it’s evaluated

  • Duplicate images removed before a stratified train / validation / test split.
  • Test images are never used for training, tuning or threshold selection.
  • Confidence thresholds are chosen on validation data only.
